Barangay Baritan Online Booking System
Terms and Conditions

I. Introduction

This document sets forth the Terms and Conditions governing the use of the Barangay Baritan Online Booking System, an official web-based service owned and managed by Barangay Baritan. The system allows residents to book appointments, facilities, or equipment free of charge, subject to barangay approval and compliance with the policies herein.

II. Eligibility

Only verified residents of Barangay Baritan are permitted to register and use the system. Users must provide accurate, up-to-date, and verifiable information when creating an account.

III. Booking Guidelines

All bookings are processed on a first-come, first-served basis and must be made through the official online platform. A booking is deemed confirmed only after barangay approval or confirmation notice. Borrowed barangay items, equipment, or facilities must be handled responsibly and returned in complete and good condition. Bookings are free of charge, but users must adhere to all barangay regulations.

IV. Sanctions and Penalties

Failure to return borrowed items, or causing damage, may result in: Account for temporary suspension or permanent suspension from the booking system; Restriction from future bookings for a period determined by barangay officials; Administrative sanctions in accordance with barangay policies; or Financial responsibility for lost or damaged barangay property.

V. Barangay Rights and Cancellations

Barangay Baritan reserves the right to cancel or modify any booking in the event of: Emergencies such as funerals or urgent community needs; Unplanned government or barangay events; or Natural calamities, including typhoons, earthquakes, or fires. In such cases, the Barangay shall make reasonable efforts to inform affected users promptly.

VI. User Obligations

Users agree to: Comply with barangay rules and regulations; Use barangay facilities and equipment responsibly; Avoid sharing their booking account with others; Ensure all activities are legal and community appropriate.

VII. Data Privacy

Barangay ensures that all personal data collected through the system will be used solely for booking verification, recordkeeping, and communication purposes, consistent with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173).

VIII. Limitation of Liability

Barangay Baritan shall not be held liable for any loss, inconvenience, or damage resulting from booking cancellations, system errors, or user misconduct.

IX. Amendments

The Barangay Baritan reserves the right to amend or revise these Terms and Conditions as necessary to improve service delivery or comply with government policies.

X. Acceptance

By using the Barangay Baritan Online Booking System, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to abide by these Terms and Conditions.

Barangay Baritan Officials
